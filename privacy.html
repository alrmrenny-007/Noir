Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

The Runway (“we”, “us”) runs the online fashion store at the-runway-mu.vercel.app. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address, and a password if you sign up with email. Passwords are encrypted before they are saved. We never store them in plain text.
  • Google sign-in: if you choose “Continue with Google”, Google shares your name and verified email address with us. We do not receive your Google password and we do not access your contacts, files or other Google data.
  • Order details: the items you buy, the amount and currency, your email, phone number and delivery address, and the status of your payment.
  • Messages: anything you send us through the contact form, the Join form, WhatsApp or email.
  • On your device: your bag, saved favourites, display preference (light or dark) and a secure login cookie are stored in your browser so the site can work.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and keep you logged in.
  • To process payments, prepare and deliver orders, and show your order history.
  • To reply to your questions and requests.
  • To protect the store against fraud and misuse.

We do not sell your personal information.

Payments

Online payments are processed by Flutterwave. Your card or bank details are entered on Flutterwave’s secure payment page. We do not see or store your full card details. Flutterwave handles that information under its own privacy policy.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the services that help us run the store: Vercel (website hosting), Neon (database), Flutterwave (payments) and Google (sign-in, if you use it). We may also share information if the law requires it.

Cookies and local storage

We use one secure, HttpOnly cookie to keep you logged in. We also use your browser’s local storage for your bag, favourites and display preference. You can clear these in your browser settings at any time, which will log you out and empty your bag.

How long we keep it

We keep your account details while your account is active, and order records for as long as we need them for business, accounting and legal reasons.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your personal information, or to close your account, by contacting us below. Where data protection law applies to you, such as Nigeria’s data protection law, you may have additional rights, and we will respect them.

Security

We use encryption, secure hosting and access controls to protect your information. No online service can be completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Children

Our store is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
